Md Sakib Hossain Shovon

Researcher & M.S. Student in Artificial Intelligence at KAIST

I am a graduate researcher at the Statistical Artificial Intelligence Lab (SAIL), KAIST AI, advised by Prof. Jaesik Choi. My research focuses on diffusion and flow matching, physical AI and world models, 3D vision, explainable AI, inference optimization, and multimodal foundation models.

About

Improving the representations, controllability, and reasoning of visual AI.

Before joining KAIST, I earned a B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from American International University-Bangladesh. My research experience includes collaborations with The University of Queensland and Qatar University. I am also the Co-Founder & CEO of NeuronTreeAI, a nonprofit initiative for AI education, research mentorship, and accessible advanced research knowledge.
