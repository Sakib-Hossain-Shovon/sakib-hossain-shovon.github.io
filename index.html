About

I am an M.S. student in Artificial Intelligence at KAIST and a researcher at the Statistical Artificial Intelligence Lab (SAIL), Kim Jaechul Graduate School of AI, South Korea, advised by Professor Jaesik Choi.

Before joining KAIST, I received my B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB). My research experience has also included work with The University of Queensland, Australia and Qatar University, Qatar.

I am also the Co-Founder & CEO of NeuronTreeAI, a nonprofit initiative co-founded with Prof. Mohammad Ali Moni focused on AI education, research mentorship, and making advanced research knowledge more accessible.

My long-term research goal is to become a professor at a top university and launch an AI startup that empowers society for a better future. My primary immediate goal is to secure a PhD position for the Fall 2027 intake at a top-10 university in the United States.

For research discussions and collaborations, contact sakib@kaist.ac.kr.
